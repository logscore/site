Is AI valuable enough that people will pay for it at cost?

You may have noticed that all your favorite AI products have been changing their pricing models. They have all decided to shift from a per-message or per-unit/credit pricing model to a usage-based model.

<!--t3.chat-->

The reason is blatantly obvious, and yet its blindsiding many people in the space. These companies are losing money very fast and it's getting harder and harder to finance the loss.
