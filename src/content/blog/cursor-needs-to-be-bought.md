# Cursor needs to be bought

Cursor is in a tough spot where they cannot afford the inference and so the plans that they give out are far less generous than the plans something like OpenAI or Anthropic give out. So they have really only two options. Either invest an immense amount in building their own data center in which they can subsidize their own inference at a loss like XAI, like Anthropic, OpenAI, or they can be purchased by one of these companies and be incorporated into that product line. I think the best option for this would be something like XAI. This is the same issue we have as I outlined in my previous blog post, [Nobody wants to pay for unsubsidized AI](/on-subsidized-inference)

This isn't unique to Cursor. There are multiple other companies which are relying on these heavily subsidized AI products, or they offer products that simply cannot compete with these subsidized AI products and therefore have to compete on a plane that does not have long term stability like the form factor for which we code using AI.
